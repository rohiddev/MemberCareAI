# Choice Plus Gold Plan Guide

## Plan Type

Choice Plus Gold is a PPO health plan.

Members may receive care from in-network and out-of-network providers, subject to the terms of the plan.

Provider network status does not by itself guarantee that a particular healthcare service is covered.

## Deductible

A deductible is the amount a member may be responsible for paying for covered healthcare services before certain plan benefits begin sharing eligible costs.

The deductible that applies to an individual member must come from the member's current benefit record.

Do not infer a member's deductible from this document.

## Coinsurance

Coinsurance is a percentage-based form of cost sharing that may apply to eligible covered services.

The percentage that applies to a particular member must come from the member's current benefit record.

A coinsurance percentage alone does not establish the final amount that a member will owe.

## Provider Network

An in-network provider participates in the plan's contracted provider network.

In-network status does not guarantee:

- coverage of a particular service
- prior authorization approval
- a specific member cost
- medical necessity determination

Members should verify benefits for the specific service when coverage details are required.

## Prior Authorization

Some healthcare services may require prior authorization.

Prior authorization requirements depend on the service, benefit configuration, and current plan rules.

Provider network status alone must not be used to determine whether prior authorization is required.
